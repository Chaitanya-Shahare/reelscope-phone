reelscope privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026.

reelscope is a personal tool used only by its author, Chaitanya Shahare, to publish his own short videos to his own Instagram account and YouTube channel and to read those accounts' own statistics. It is not offered to anyone else.

Google and YouTube data

reelscope uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API, with the author's own Google account, to upload videos to the author's channel and to read the channel's public statistics and analytics (views, watch time, subscribers gained). It does not access any other user's data. The sign-in token is stored on the author's own computer and in his private GitHub repositories as encrypted secrets. Data is never sold, shared or used for advertising. reelscope's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube's terms: youtube.com/t/terms; Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
